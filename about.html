About

Mind Wank is an “artist” who uses “AI.” His films include You Are, Unfortunately, an A.I. Artist, which won the Best Narrative Award at Runway's Gen:48 (second edition, 2024), A.I. WILL NOT REPLACE US, THE LAST BAYESIAN, and Newt, an award-winning mockumentary. His print Equus was exhibited at Les Rencontres d'Arles in 2023. He makes stills and videos, sells some of them, and answers his own email.
